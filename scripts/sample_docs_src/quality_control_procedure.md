# Quality Control Procedure

Panelvolt d.o.o., Novi Sad · Document: QP-07 · Revision C · Effective date: 15 January 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure defines how Panelvolt inspects incoming materials, monitors production and verifies finished low-voltage switchgear assemblies before delivery. It applies to all products manufactured in production halls A and B, including the PV-400 cabinet system.

## 2. Responsibilities

- Quality Manager (Ana Jovanović): owns this procedure, approves deviations and "use as is" decisions, and leads customer complaint investigations.
- QC Inspector: performs incoming, in-process and final inspections and records the results.
- Production Lead: makes sure that no product leaves a work station without the required inspection stamp.
- Warehouse: keeps quarantined material in the quarantine area Q1 until a decision is made.

## 3. Incoming inspection

Every delivery is checked against the purchase order and the delivery note. Certificates of conformity (CoC) are required for circuit breakers, contactors and copper busbars; deliveries without a certificate are placed in quarantine.

- Electrical components: sampling according to ISO 2859-1, general inspection level II, AQL 1.0.
- Copper busbars: 100% visual inspection for scratches, oxidation and correct dimensions.
- Enclosures and sheet metal parts: 10% sample for dimensions and paint thickness (minimum 80 µm).

Rejected material is marked with a red tag and moved to the quarantine area Q1 within the same shift.

## 4. In-process inspection

In-process inspections are performed at two hold points:

- After mechanical assembly: frame dimensions, mounting of devices according to the layout drawing, torque of busbar connections. Every tightened busbar bolt must be marked with a yellow paint marker.
- After wiring: wire cross-sections and colours, wire markers according to the wiring diagram, routing and strain relief.

The QC Inspector signs and stamps the traveller sheet after each hold point. Work may not continue to the next stage without the stamp.

## 5. Final inspection and routine tests

Each assembly undergoes routine verification according to IEC 61439-1 before it is released for delivery. The results are recorded in the Test Report form QF-07-03.

| Test | Method | Acceptance criterion |
| Visual inspection | Check against drawings and bill of materials | No deviations |
| Protective circuit continuity | Low-resistance ohmmeter, 10 A | ≤ 0.1 Ω |
| Insulation resistance | 500 V DC for 60 s | ≥ 1 MΩ |
| Dielectric test | 2500 V AC, 50 Hz, for 1 s | No breakdown or flashover |
| Functional test | Simulation of control circuits | All functions as specified |

Dielectric tests may only be performed in the test bay by authorised personnel. A red warning lamp above the test bay entrance must be switched on during the test.

If any test fails, the assembly is marked with a red tag, the failure is recorded in the test report and a nonconformity report is opened (see section 6). After repair, the complete set of routine tests is repeated.

## 6. Nonconformities

A nonconformity report (NCR) must be opened within 24 hours of detecting a nonconformity. The NCR records the product, the description of the problem, the immediate action and the proposed disposition.

Possible dispositions are:

- Rework: the product is repaired and inspected again.
- Scrap: the product or part is destroyed and recorded as scrap.
- Use as is: only with written approval of the Quality Manager and, for customer orders, of the customer.

## 7. Customer complaints

Customer complaints are handled with the 8D method. The customer receives a first response (containment action) within 2 working days and the complete 8D report within 10 working days.

## 8. Records

Test reports, NCRs and 8D reports are archived electronically in the quality management system and retained for 10 years after delivery of the product.
